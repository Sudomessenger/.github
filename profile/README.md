## What is Sudo?

Sudo is a **non-custodial Web3 messenger** built around wallet identity, encrypted peer chat, and on-chain coordination. Messaging, payments, and trust live in one surface — without giving up custody of keys or readable message content to a central operator.

| Pillar | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Wallet-native identity** | Sign in with your wallet. Your keys stay on your device. |
| **Encrypted messaging** | P2P chat, groups, channels, and broadcasts with privacy-first design. |
| **In-line Web3 actions** | Send tokens, swap, bridge, and interact with dApps without leaving chat. |
| **Validator-secured deals** | Escrow milestones, dispute resolution, and on-chain settlement flows. |
| **Voice & video** | Meetings and live coordination inside the same messenger surface. |

## Core capabilities

- **Non-custodial Sudo Wallet** — multi-chain, seed phrases never leave the device
- **WalletConnect / Reown AppKit** — connect MetaMask, Trust, Coinbase, Rainbow, and 590+ wallets
- **Smart groups & channels** — on-chain group logic, broadcasts, and community surfaces
- **SUDO mining** — pair-to-earn incentives tied to network participation
- **Escrow & swaps** — in-chat payments, bridges, and validator-backed settlement
- **Web3 tools & bots** — audits, transaction tracking, chain/token helpers, and mini-apps
- **Stories & rich media** — images, video, voice notes, files, and live updates

## ✦ What is Sudo?

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Sudo** is a non-custodial Web3 messenger where wallet identity, encrypted chat, and on-chain coordination live in one surface.

You sign in with your wallet, keep keys on-device, and move from conversation to payment to settlement without switching apps.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Built for operators and users alike**

- Wallet-native login & SIWE-style auth  
- Encrypted P2P chat, groups & channels  
- In-chat swaps, bridges & escrow  
- Voice, video & rich media stories  
- Validator-backed dispute resolution  

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## ✦ Core capabilities

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**💼 Sudo Wallet**  
Multi-chain, non-custodial wallet — seed phrases never leave the device.

**🔗 WalletConnect / Reown**  
MetaMask, Trust, Coinbase, Rainbow, OKX, SafePal & 590+ wallets.

**🏛️ Smart groups & channels**  
On-chain group logic, broadcasts, and community surfaces.

**⛏️ SUDO mining**  
Pair-to-earn incentives tied to network participation.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**🤝 Escrow & swaps**  
In-chat payments, bridges, and validator-backed settlement.

**🤖 Web3 tools & bots**  
Audits, transaction tracking, chain/token helpers, mini-apps.

**📸 Stories & rich media**  
Images, video, voice notes, files, and live updates.

**🔍 Trust & safety**  
Validator queue, contract tools, and encrypted-state operations.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
